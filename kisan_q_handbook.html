📌 Executive Summary & Vision

KISAN-Q is a high-resilience rural queue management and gate-logistics infrastructure engineered specifically for Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) Mandis. Traditional appointment apps fail in rural India because they assume reliable 5G internet, high smartphone literacy, and predictable highway conditions. KISAN-Q is built from the ground up for rural realities: 70% feature phone adoption, massive tractor gluts during peak harvest, sudden unseasonal rain, and frequent rural telecom outages.

⚙️ System Architecture & Technology Stack

  • Backend: Ultra-fast Node.js & Express REST API running as an on-premise Mandi Edge Server.
  • Frontend: High-contrast, mobile-first responsive HTML5, modern Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript (zero heavy framework overhead).
  • Voice AI & Audio: Web Speech Synthesis API (multi-dialect Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi) + Web Audio API synthesizing authentic dual-tone multi-frequency (DTMF) telecom beeps.
  • Offline Cryptography: Cryptographic SHA-256 HMAC digital signatures embedded into QR passes, verifiable completely offline.
  • State Engine: High-throughput in-memory state engine designed for sub-millisecond edge response, eliminating cloud database timeout vulnerabilities.

Detailed Breakdown of Each Application Page

Walkthrough of Features, Workflows, and Embedded Logic

🌾 Page 1: Farmer Smart Booking Portal (tab-farmer)

Primary Purpose: Allows farmers and village CSC (Common Service Center) operators to pre-book gate arrival slots using a clean, accessible interface.

Key Functional Components:

  1. Crop & MSP Benchmark: Farmers select their crop (Wheat, Paddy, Mustard, Gram, Soybean). The system dynamically fetches official Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) benchmarks and auto-computes estimated payouts.
  2. Fair-Share Weighted Allocation Algorithm:
    Fair-Share Logic: Marginal and small farmers owning under 2 hectares are prioritized in the queue. This structural bias guarantees smallholder farmers are not pushed to late-night slots by wealthy traders with bulk fleets.
  3. Predictive Turnaround Time (TAT) Engine: Calculates expected gate-to-exit turnaround time based on vehicle type (Tractor Trolley: 35 min, Pickup/Bolero: 20 min, 10-Wheeler Truck: 55 min) and live weighbridge queue depth.
  4. Instant QR Digital Gate Pass: Generates an instant high-resolution QR pass displaying Token Number (e.g., KT-1049), Assigned Gate, Arrival Window, and a cryptographic verification stamp.
  5. On-Ground Realism: Embedded documentary visual showcasing an Indian farmer proudly receiving his digital mandi gate pass.

📱 Page 2: Zero-Smartphone Nokia IVR Simulator (tab-ivr)

Primary Purpose: Bridges the digital divide for the 70% of Indian farmers who carry basic ₹800 keypad phones without internet.

Interactive Workflow:

  1. Toll-Free Missed Call Trigger: Farmer gives a missed call to toll-free number 1800-547-2600.
  2. Automated IVR Callback: The Mandi automated IVR system calls back immediately in the farmer's preferred regional language (Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi).
  3. Interactive Retro Nokia 3310 Keypad: Judges can physically click the realistic phone buttons. Pressing keys triggers authentic DTMF tone beeps and advances the voice menu:
    • Press 1: Select Wheat / Rice
    • Press 2: Select Morning / Afternoon Slot
    • Press #: Confirm Booking
  4. Voice AI Synthesis: Speaks out loud using the browser's native Web Speech synthesis engine.
  5. Automated SMS Receipt: Once confirmed, the phone screen displays the incoming SMS gate pass containing the Token Number and Entry Gate.

🛰️ Page 3: IMD Doppler Weather Resilience (tab-weather)

Primary Purpose: Prevents post-harvest crop destruction caused by unseasonal rain while grain is queued outside the mandi.

Disaster Mitigation Engine:

  • Telemetry Integration: Simulates live Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) Doppler radar telemetry displaying cloud cover, precipitation probability, and wind shear.
  • The "Simulate Severe Rain Warning" Trigger: When triggered, the system automatically:
    1. Halts un-dispatched tractor departures from villages.
    2. Sends urgent voice/SMS broadcasts to farmers on the road to head directly to Covered Silo Complex #3.
    3. Displays documentary proof of moisture-proof tarpaulin silos and lab moisture testing.
    4. Demonstrates an immediate saving of ₹2,40,000 in prevented crop damage for a single batch of 18 tractors.

💡 Top 5 Technical & Operational Judge Questions (Instant Answers)

Q1: "Where is your database? Did you connect MongoDB or SQL?"

Answer: “Sir/Ma'am, for this field prototype we intentionally engineered an In-Memory High-Throughput State Engine with Local Edge Persistence. In rural mandis, cloud databases fail when optical fiber is cut. Local state guarantees sub-millisecond gate responses and 100% offline uptime. In Phase 2 national scaling, our repository layer maps directly to PostgreSQL via Prisma ORM.”

Q2: "How does the IVR system connect in real production?"

Answer: “Our browser prototype demonstrates the interactive DTMF keypad and speech synthesis logic. In national deployment, our Node.js backend hooks via webhooks into telecom cloud gateways like Exotel or Twilio, utilizing toll-free missed call PRI lines to trigger automated voice callbacks.”

Q3: "How does offline gate verification work without internet?"

Answer: “The gate pass carries a SHA-256 HMAC cryptographic signature hashed with the local mandi's daily private secret key, farmer registration number, and timestamp. The guard's handheld terminal verifies the mathematical signature locally without querying any remote server.”

Q4: "What prevents rich traders from booking all the morning slots?"

Answer: “Our Fair-Share Weighted Allocation Algorithm reserves 60% of prime early-morning slots exclusively for marginal farmers owning under 2 hectares, preventing commercial bulk fleets from monopolizing peak mandi hours.”

Q5: "Did you use AI in this project?"

Answer: “Yes! We integrated AI across three layers: Voice AI (Text-to-Speech synthesis in regional dialects), Predictive Turnaround Time heuristics, and Automated Weather Risk Assessment. We also used modern AI developer tooling to rapidly scaffold production-ready code.”
